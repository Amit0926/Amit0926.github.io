About

Operations, technology, and customer experience — one workflow.

I'm an E-commerce Operations Manager with 4+ years running high-volume operations at Giftsvilla Private Limited, the company behind the personalized-gifting brand giftsbyrashi.com.

I own the complete order-to-delivery process: order processing, personalization coordination, packaging quality checks, dispatch, courier coordination, vendor sourcing, and customer support — for a team of 10–15 people processing 200+ orders a day.

Alongside operations, I conceptualized and built luxehampers.com from scratch, and use web development and AI automation to remove repetitive work from the day-to-day — so the team spends time on orders, not on manual admin.
